## Overview

Luxdata is a complete hotel management system built to streamline daily hotel operations — from the initial reservation through checkout and billing.

## Problem

Managing rooms by hand caused booking conflicts, billing errors, and no visibility into real occupancy. The operations team needed a single unified tool.

## Solution

A web platform with integrated modules for each operational area:

- **Reservations**: create, modify and cancel with real-time availability checks
- **Check-in / Check-out**: guided flow with guest registration
- **Billing**: invoice generation including add-on services
- **Room blocking**: maintenance, cleaning and special states
- **Dashboards**: current occupancy, revenue for the period, geographic distribution of guests

## Key metrics

- 82 rooms managed
- 64 rooms active in high season
- 18% increase in reported revenue vs. the previous system
- 12 simultaneous guest check-ins with no conflicts

## Tech stack

- **Frontend**: React, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: MySQL with transactions for reservation consistency
